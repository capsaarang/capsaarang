### About

I build products for systems that can't afford to break.

I started on the engineering side. At **NPCI**, I worked on the infrastructure behind India's national payments system, simulating **25,000+ concurrent transactions** and automating anomaly detection that replaced 3 manual analyst roles. At **ArisGlobal**, I built a BI product for pharma clients like J&J and AstraZeneca that every VP in the company adopted, saving **$150K a year** and earning a Spot Award.

Then I moved into product. As a **Technical Product Manager Intern at Marketeq Digital** (Jun to Aug 2026), I owned a checkout flow end to end: 87+ user stories and 50+ edge cases, from spec to release.

Now I'm building AI products. In finance, an AI that is confidently wrong is worse than no AI at all.

**MS Information Science @ UW-Madison (4.0)** · Graduate TA, CS 252 · CFA candidate

### Projects

| Project | Problem, decision, outcome | Links |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Forensic-AI** | **Problem:** red flags in 10-K filings hide across hundreds of pages, and generic AI tools hallucinate citations. **Decision:** RAG over fine-tuning, because filings change every quarter. **Outcome:** a 6-stage agentic pipeline that pulls filings from SEC EDGAR and returns a 0 to 100 risk score with cited evidence. | [Repo](https://github.com/capsaarang/forensic-ai/tree/main/forensic-ai) |
| **AI PRD Generator** | **Problem:** PRDs take PMs hours, and their structure varies by team. **Decision:** Claude API with structured prompting instead of a fine-tuned model, so teams can adapt the format. **Outcome:** user stories, acceptance criteria, edge cases, and success metrics from a raw feature request in about 60 seconds. | [Live](https://ai-prd-generator-dsx5lesekggb5pj8k7gbsf.streamlit.app) · [Repo](https://github.com/capsaarang/ai-prd-generator) |
| **CareerCraft** | **Problem:** tailoring a resume to every job description takes hours. **Decision:** generate LaTeX instead of Word, so formatting stays locked while the content changes, with one click to open in Overleaf. **Outcome:** a full-stack platform that tailors resumes, tracks applications, and preps interviews. | [Repo](https://github.com/capsaarang/career-craft) |
